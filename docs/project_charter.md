# 1. Executive Summary

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by telecommunications companies. Every customer who leaves represents lost revenue, increased customer acquisition costs, and reduced market competitiveness.

This project aims to develop an end-to-end Machine Learning model capable of predicting whether a customer is likely to leave a telecom service provider based on demographic information, subscribed services, account history, and billing behaviour.

The predictive model will enable telecom companies to identify high-risk customers early and implement proactive retention strategies that improve customer satisfaction and reduce financial losses.

This project demonstrates the complete Machine Learning lifecycle, including business understanding, data acquisition, exploratory data analysis, data preprocessing, feature engineering, model development, evaluation, explainability, deployment, and documentation.

# 2. Business Problem

Telecommunication companies invest significant resources in acquiring new customers. However, retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.

Customer churn occurs when subscribers discontinue their services and migrate to competitors. High churn rates reduce profitability, weaken customer loyalty, and negatively affect long-term business growth.

Without an effective predictive system, companies only react after customers have already left. This project seeks to shift that approach from reactive to proactive by predicting customer churn before it happens.

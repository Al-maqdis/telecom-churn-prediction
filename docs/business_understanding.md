# Business Understanding

---

# 1. Project Background

The telecommunications industry is one of the most competitive sectors globally. Service providers continuously invest significant resources in acquiring new customers while facing increasing customer attrition due to competition, pricing, service quality, and customer experience.

Customer churn—the loss of existing subscribers—directly affects revenue, profitability, and long-term business sustainability. Predicting customers who are likely to leave before they actually churn enables organizations to implement proactive retention strategies, reduce customer loss, and improve overall customer satisfaction.

This project develops a Machine Learning solution capable of predicting customer churn using historical customer information and service usage patterns.

---

# 2. Business Goal

The primary business goal is to reduce customer churn by identifying customers who are at high risk of leaving the telecom service provider.

The predictive insights generated from this project can support customer retention campaigns, improve decision-making, and increase customer lifetime value.

---

# 3. Business Questions

This project seeks to answer the following questions:

- Which customers are most likely to churn?
- Which customer characteristics contribute most to churn?
- What services or contract types are associated with higher churn rates?
- Which customer segments require proactive retention strategies?
- How accurately can Machine Learning predict customer churn?

---

# 4. Stakeholders

The primary stakeholders include:

- Telecom Company Management
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Team
- Marketing Department
- Customer Retention Team
- Business Intelligence Analysts
- Data Science Team
- Executive Decision Makers

Each stakeholder benefits from accurate churn predictions that support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 5. Project Scope

This project focuses on developing a supervised Machine Learning classification model using historical telecom customer data.

The project covers:

- Business understanding
- Data acquisition
- Data validation
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data preprocessing
- Feature engineering
- Model training
- Model comparison
- Hyperparameter tuning
- Model evaluation
- Model explainability
- Model deployment using Streamlit
- Documentation and reporting

The project does not include integration into a live telecom production environment.

---

# 6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Produces a reliable churn prediction model.
- Demonstrates strong predictive performance using appropriate evaluation metrics.
- Identifies key business drivers influencing churn.
- Provides actionable recommendations for customer retention.
- Is fully reproducible and well documented.
- Can be deployed through an interactive Streamlit application.

---

# 7. Assumptions

The following assumptions are made:

- Historical customer behaviour is predictive of future churn.
- The dataset accurately represents customer behaviour.
- Missing values and inconsistencies can be appropriately handled.
- Customer attributes included in the dataset are sufficient for predictive modelling.
